Put your background music here.

1. Download a royalty-free LOFI loop (something you have the rights to). Good free sources:
   - Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) — search "lofi", filter by license
   - YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library)
   - Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org)
   - Chosic (chosic.com/free-music/lofi)

2. Rename the file to exactly:  lofi.mp3
   (an .ogg also works if you rename the <audio> source; mp3 is easiest)

3. Drop it in this "music" folder.

The ♪ LOFI button in the gallery will light up and play it (looping, with a gentle fade).
Until a file is here, the button just stays dimmed.
